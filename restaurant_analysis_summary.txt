12. MENU-PLANNING RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Keep the highest-quantity dishes adequately stocked and monitor inventory before peak ordering periods.
2. Review the highest-revenue menu category and test complementary items or bundles using actual sales data.
3. Align kitchen preparation and staffing with the busiest hour and weekday identified in the dataset.
4. Compare average bills across dine-in, takeaway and delivery before designing service-mode promotions.
5. Review unusually large order bills individually; an IQR outlier is a statistical flag, not automatically an error.

LIMITATION
All records are synthetic sample data created for this assignment. Findings demonstrate the analysis workflow and must not be treated as actual restaurant sales.
